SCENARIO: Recover After An Awkward Moment

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Acknowledges the awkward moment briefly without overexplaining
- Resets tone and moves the conversation forward
- Does not grovel, spiral into apology, or pretend nothing happened
- Tone after the recovery sounds natural, not stiff or defensive

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Over-apologizes ("I'm so sorry, I didn't mean it like that, I just...")
- Tries to explain the joke or comment in a way that makes it weirder
- Pretends nothing happened (which often lands as worse)
- Goes silent or shuts down

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Uses a small bit of warmth or honest humor to fully reset the room
- Names the awkwardness with confidence rather than shrinking from it
