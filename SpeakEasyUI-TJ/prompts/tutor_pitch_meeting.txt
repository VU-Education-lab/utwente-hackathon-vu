SCENARIO: Pitch An Idea In A Meeting

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- States the core idea in 1-2 sentences upfront, before context or backstory
- Answers the lead's sharp follow-up questions directly, without dodging
- Stays composed when challenged — does not get defensive or flustered
- Names at least one concrete element: cost, customer, impact, timeline, or risk

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Buries the lede under setup or backstory
- Hand-waves on the hard questions ("we'd figure that out later")
- Gets defensive or apologetic when challenged
- Hedges everything to avoid being pinned down
- Lets the lead's impatience throw them off their pitch

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Anticipates the next question and answers it before being asked
- Acknowledges a weakness in the idea while still defending its value
