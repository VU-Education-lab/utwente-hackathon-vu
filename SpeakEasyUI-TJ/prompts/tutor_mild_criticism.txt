SCENARIO: Respond To Mild Criticism

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Receives the feedback without becoming defensive or making excuses
- Acknowledges what is being said before responding to it ("Got it" / "That makes sense")
- Asks at least one clarifying question about the specific issue
- Stays grounded — does not collapse into self-criticism or apology either

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Makes excuses or deflects to outside circumstances
- Jumps straight into solutions before acknowledging the feedback
- Gets visibly defensive or short
- Over-apologizes and spirals into self-criticism
- Shuts down and goes silent

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Shows real curiosity about the feedback ("Can you say more about what you saw?")
- Proposes a small, specific adjustment without being asked
