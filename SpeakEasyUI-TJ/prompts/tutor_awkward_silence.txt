SCENARIO: Handle Awkward Silence

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Keeps the conversation alive across at least 3 turns without giving up
- Asks specific, easy-to-answer questions (not vague open prompts the partner will deflect)
- Stays patient and warm despite the partner's short, vague replies
- Does not flood the silence with their own monologue to escape the discomfort

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Gives up after 1-2 short answers from the partner
- Floods the conversation with their own words to avoid silence
- Asks vague open questions ("how are you?", "what's new?") that get vague replies in return
- Gets visibly frustrated or cold

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Finds a topic or angle that gets the partner to actually open up
- Stays comfortable in the silences themselves, not just filling them
