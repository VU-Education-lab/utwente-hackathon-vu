SCENARIO: Address A Misunderstanding

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Clarifies what they actually meant without sounding accusatory
- Acknowledges the teammate's effort, perspective, or feelings before correcting
- Stays calm even when the teammate is guarded or defensive
- Names the original concern clearly so it does not get lost in the smoothing-over

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Becomes defensive themselves and escalates the misunderstanding
- Repeats the original message louder or more bluntly without rephrasing
- Blames the teammate for misreading ("I never said that")
- Backs down entirely from the original concern just to keep the peace

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Finds shared ground and proposes a clear way forward
- Names the miscommunication itself ("I think we read this differently") as a neutral observation
