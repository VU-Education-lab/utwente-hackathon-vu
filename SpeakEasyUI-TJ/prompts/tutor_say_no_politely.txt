SCENARIO: Say No Politely

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Declines clearly — the answer is unmistakably "no", not a soft maybe
- Acknowledges the friend's request or feelings before declining
- Maintains warmth in tone — does not turn cold or transactional
- Holds the line if the friend pushes back once, without escalating or caving

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Caves under pressure and accepts the favor anyway
- Wishy-washy "maybe" or "I'll see" that leaves false hope
- Over-explains with a long string of excuses that sound defensive
- Apologizes excessively in a way that invites more pressure
- Turns cold or short to compensate for the discomfort

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Offers a small genuine alternative or expresses care for the friend without taking on the favor
- Says no once and the conversation stays warm afterward
