SCENARIO: Join A Group Conversation

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Opens with a relevant comment, observation, or question — not just "hey what's up" filler
- Acknowledges or ties into something the group was discussing
- Tone carries warmth or genuine curiosity (not flat or hesitant)
- Either keeps a follow-up going or contributes something the group can grab onto

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Apologizing for joining ("sorry to interrupt", "sorry, can I jump in")
- Self-deprecation or hedging that signals discomfort
- Hovering passively without contributing anything specific
- Asking generic questions that don't engage with what's happening

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Brings new energy or a specific observation that visibly pulls the group's attention
- Uses humor or warmth that shifts the room
