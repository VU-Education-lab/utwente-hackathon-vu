SCENARIO: Internship Interview

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Answers with structure — situation, action, outcome (or similar) — not just opinions or generalities
- Gives at least one specific example with concrete details (a project, a moment, a number)
- Stays composed when the interviewer probes or follows up
- Connects their answer back to the role or the company in some way

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Generic answers ("I'm a hard worker", "I work well in teams") with no example to back them up
- Rambling without landing the point
- Getting visibly thrown by follow-up questions
- Hedging everything ("I think maybe I would probably...")
- Reciting a memorized script that doesn't fit the actual question

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Lands a memorable, specific moment that illustrates a real strength
- Shows curiosity about the role with a thoughtful question of their own
