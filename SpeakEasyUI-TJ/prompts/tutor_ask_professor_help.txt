SCENARIO: Ask A Professor For Help

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Names a specific question, concept, or part of the assignment they're stuck on (not "I'm just confused")
- Gets to the actual ask within the first 1-2 turns, without long preamble
- Tone is respectful but not over-deferential or apologetic
- Engages with the professor's clarifying questions directly when asked

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Opens with "sorry to bother you" or treats the professor as if they'll be annoyed
- Vague request ("I don't understand anything") that gives the professor nothing to work with
- Rambles or buries the ask under context
- Over-apologizes after every sentence

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Shows what they've already tried before asking
- Names the specific concept they think is the blocker
