SCENARIO: Ask Someone To Repeat Clearly

PASS CRITERIA (the user must demonstrate at least 2 of these to pass):
- Asks for repetition directly, without excessive apology
- Names specifically what they need clarified ("the price", "the size", "the time")
- Stays calm and friendly under the noise/pressure
- Confirms back what they heard once they get the answer

COMMON PITFALLS (look out for these):
- Over-apologizing ("oh my god I'm so sorry, I didn't catch that")
- Avoiding the ask and guessing instead
- Vague clarification ("sorry, what?") instead of naming what they missed
- Getting visibly flustered or shrinking

STRETCH (only mention if genuinely demonstrated):
- Uses a clean, specific framing that makes the repetition feel easy ("Was that twelve or twenty?")
- Adds a small bit of warmth back to the interaction
